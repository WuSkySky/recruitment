**簡短介紹版 / Short Introduction：**  
AzureSky 機器人戰隊成立於 2025 年 5 月，主要參加中國及亞洲地區的機器人賽事與技術交流活動。戰隊希望讓更多年輕人參與真實的工程專案，在設計、製作與競賽中成長為機器人工程師。  
The AzureSky Robotics Team was founded in May 2025. It primarily participates in robotics competitions and technical exchange activities in China and across Asia. The team aims to give more young people hands-on experience with real engineering projects and help them grow into robotics engineers through design, fabrication, and competition.

# 招新安排 / Recruitment Plan

## 招新理念 / Recruitment Philosophy

### 熱愛比基礎更重要 / Passion Matters More Than Prior Experience

我們不要求你在加入前就具備完整的知識體系或比賽經驗。我們更看重你對機器人的真正興趣、主動學習的意願，以及面對問題時願意繼續嘗試的態度。不要因為「沒有基礎」而提前放棄，基礎可以在實踐中逐步建立。  
We do not expect you to have a complete body of knowledge or competition experience before joining. What matters more is your genuine interest in robotics, your willingness to learn proactively, and your determination to keep trying when problems arise. Do not give up simply because you lack prior experience—you can build a strong foundation step by step through practice.

**沒有經驗不是問題，願意學、願意做更重要。 / A lack of experience is not a problem; the willingness to learn and take action matters more.**

### 招新是一次雙向選擇 / Recruitment Is a Two-Way Choice

戰隊會瞭解你的興趣、學習意願與協作方式；你也需要瞭解戰隊的工作內容、時間投入和協作節奏。面試的目的是讓雙方充分瞭解，判斷彼此是否適合長期合作。  
The team will learn about your interests, willingness to learn, and approach to collaboration. You should also understand the team’s work, time commitment, and pace of collaboration. The interview allows both sides to get to know each other and determine whether they are a good fit for long-term teamwork.

**我們在尋找合適的隊友，你也在選擇適合自己的團隊。 / We are looking for the right teammates, and you are choosing the team that is right for you.**
